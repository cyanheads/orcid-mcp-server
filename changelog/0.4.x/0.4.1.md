# 0.4.1 — 2026-11-16

## Added

- **`orcid_resolve_researcher`** candidates carry `otherNames`, and one `notice` joins the cap, anchor-only, and ranking parts. The anchor-only sentence names every anchor supplied and says the candidates were found by it alone, and the `rows` step appears while `rows` is below min(`totalFound`, 20) ([#85](https://github.com/cyanheads/orcid-mcp-server/issues/85)).
- **`orcid_get_funding`** detail returns `contributors` (`name`, `orcidId`, `role`; `[]` when none was deposited) and `organizationDefinedType` ([#86](https://github.com/cyanheads/orcid-mcp-server/issues/86)).
- **`orcid_get_profile`** returns `emails[].verified`, and the opt-in `include_history` reads `/record` in place of `/person` (still one request) and returns `history`. For a heavy publisher the upstream response can exceed 10 MB and the first, uncached call can time out; call again ([#87](https://github.com/cyanheads/orcid-mcp-server/issues/87)).

## Fixed

- **`orcid_search_researchers` and `orcid_resolve_researcher`** match a `doi` anchor as one `doi-self` regex term, in any ASCII letter case, bare or behind `https://doi.org/`, `dx.`, or `doi:` ([#84](https://github.com/cyanheads/orcid-mcp-server/issues/84)).
- **`orcid_get_research_resources` and `orcid_get_work_detail`**: when the `/person` merge check cannot complete (anything but a 404 or caller cancellation), the answer stands and says so. The notice opens with the disclosure, and the `put_code` `resource_not_found` message carries it. The check waits at most 10 s and never past the call deadline ([#83](https://github.com/cyanheads/orcid-mcp-server/issues/83)).
- **`orcid_resolve_researcher`** compares a dash both ways in names (`Jean-Pierre` = `Jean Pierre`, `Min-Jun` = `Minjun`), and a dashed word's parts match on either side, so dashed names that classified `none` now reach `exact`, `partial`, or `other-name`.
- **`orcid_resolve_researcher`** `structuredContent.notice` carries the caller's name as typed; Markdown escaping happens only when the notice renders into `content[]`.
